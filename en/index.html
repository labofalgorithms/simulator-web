about

Made in the classroom, open to everyone.

LabOfAlgorithms is a project by Bruno de A. Iizuka Moritani, who teaches Algorithms and Data Structures. The idea is simple: instead of imagining what happens in memory, you run each step, follow the pseudocode and watch the variables change.

StudentsPractice at your own paceStep forward and back and test your own data, with no sign-up and nothing to install.
TeachersUse it in classTexts and pseudocode under CC BY-NC 4.0: adapt and share with credit, for non-commercial use.
ContactGet in touchFound a mistake or have a suggestion? Write to contact@labofalgorithms.com or open an issue on GitHub.
modular architecture

One engine, many simulators.

The step controller, autoplay, theme and storage are shared. Each structure provides only its own algorithms, parameters and visual representation.

CoreSimulationControllerRun, pause, step forward, step back and control the speed.
ModuleAlgorithmsGeneration of the states and explanations of each operation.
RendererVisualizationArray, matrix, stack, queue, list, sorting, tree or graph.
